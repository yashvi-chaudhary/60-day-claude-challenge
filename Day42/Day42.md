Today's build: a Personal Financial Command Center — but not another expense tracker.

Most student budgeting tools just log transactions. I wanted something that actually tells you where you stand and what to do next.

What it does:
 🎯 Computes a live financial health score — from savings rate, budget discipline, and goal progress, not a vibe
 📊 Runs "what-if" simulations — cut a category or add income, see the ripple before you commit
 🫙 The piece I'm proudest of: a savings goal jar that fills like liquid as you log money toward a real target
 🤖 Surfaces AI insights straight from your actual numbers — no generic advice

The uncomfortable finding: building the health score forced me to admit that "tracking spending" and "improving financial health" are different problems. A tracker tells you what happened. A health score has to make a judgment call about what's good — and that's a much harder design decision than it looks.

Single HTML file. Zero dependencies. Everything stays in the browser via local storage.

Built entirely with Claude — from architecture to the jar-fill animation.
