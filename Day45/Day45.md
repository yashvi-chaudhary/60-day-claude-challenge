Day 45/60: I built a Decision Report instead of a to-do list

Most days of this challenge I've shipped a tool. Today I turned Claude into something different — an impartial Decision Strategist that interviews you first, then builds your report.

The build: A guided 4-question interview (goal, timeline, gut instinct, biggest fear) → feeds into a single-file interactive HTML dashboard with a decision matrix, premortem analysis, a 7-day test plan, and a verdict — animated bars, dark glassmorphism, the works.

The uncomfortable finding: I ran my own final-year dilemma through it — "keep building AI skills" vs "focus on interview readiness." My gut said keep learning. The report said otherwise: my blocker wasn't skill, it was rehearsal. No amount of extra learning fixes a confidence problem that only shows up when you're being asked questions out loud.

Domain logic > UI polish, again. The hardest part wasn't the CSS animations — it was making sure every matrix score was actually justified by what I said, not invented to sound clever.

Sometimes the most useful thing you can build isn't a feature. It's a mirror.
