Day 44 of #60DayClaudeChallenge: I built a tool to roast my own LinkedIn profile — and it wasn't fun.

Today's build: an interactive LinkedIn Profile Auditor. You feed it your actual headline, About section, experience, and skills — and it scores every section like a recruiter would, then rebuilds it line by line.

I ran mine through it first. The uncomfortable finding: my profile scored 32/100, not because my writing was bad — because there was almost nothing written at all. No real headline. No About section. Two solid projects (a fraud detection ML model, a full Java student management system) sitting completely off my profile.

The tool doesn't just flag problems — it rewrites the headline (3 strategic versions), the About section, and turns vague bullet points into achievement-driven ones, before-and-after, so you can see exactly what changed and why.
Lesson from today: most students aren't losing opportunities because they lack skills. They're losing them because nothing on their profile says what those skills are.

Built with Claude. Fixed my own profile with it in the same sitting.
